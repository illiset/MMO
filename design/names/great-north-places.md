# The Great North — place names

Names for every territory's main settlement and every start point marked on
`design/Mythic Earth-Great North.jpg` (2k twin committed beside it).

**Status: APPROVED — Daniel, 2026-09-12.** All 55 names locked; this is canon for
world building. Authored in an Opus design session from
`design/briefs/2026-09-12-naming-great-north.md`. Machine twin:
`data/zones/great-north-names.json`. Rename only on Daniel's say-so.

**Already on the map (not renamed):** Tara (Sidhe seat), Dál Riata (the Celtic
island/zone), Miklagarðr and Hedeby (Germanic), Crusader Empire, Italics
Romana, Thracian Frontier, Castrated Plains, Dew Hollow.

---

## How the map allocates starts

Read off the 8k export, territory by territory. Start markers are labelled by
ARCHETYPE, and some are combined — a territory gets between one and four
markers depending on which archetypes its races can actually roll:

| Markers | Territories |
|---|---|
| 4 separate | Celtic, Hellenic |
| 3, one combined | Sidhe, Germanic, Alfar, Baltic, Slavic, Romance, Armenian |
| 2, both combined | Fae |
| 2 | Woodling |
| 1 | Hillback, Aethiopes, Mythic, Gobbledrift, Centaur |

Every territory covers every archetype its races can actually roll — confirmed
against `data/factions/great-north.json` and with Daniel on 2026-09-12. The
apparent gaps are all real class gaps, not missing markers:

- **Woodling has no Frontline start** because Woodling has no Frontline class.
- **Centaur has no Support start** because Centaur has no Support class.
- **Gobbledrift's single start** is Support + DPS, the only two archetypes it rolls.
- **Fae's two markers each carry two archetypes** — Healer rides with Frontline,
  Damage rides with Support.
- **Mythic, Hillback and Aethiopes** roll all four and funnel them through one gate.

Naming conventions used throughout: real linguistic morphology, invented
compounds. No lifted modern town names; no DAoC, WoW or Warhammer places. Every
name is unique across the realm so players can say it in chat without ambiguity.

## 1. Celtic — Dál Riata *(first zone, 3 km tip to tip)*

Old Irish / Gaelic. The island reads north-cape → hill-road → ford → southern gate.

**Settlement: Dunadd** — the royal seat on the crag. The rock-cut footprint at its
gate is where every Dál Riata king has stood. *(Approved 2026-09-12; the
alternate **Dunlaith** was declined.)*

| Start | Name | Say it | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frontline | **Lissban** | LISS-bawn | "The White Enclosure" — the lime-washed muster yard below Dunadd's southern gate, where the warbands form before they ever see the sea. |
| Damage | **Rinnbeg** | RIN-beg | "The Little Point" — a hunters' camp on the north cape, close enough to the cliff edge that the gulls wake you. |
| Healer | **Tobarglas** | TOH-bar-glass | "The Green Well" — a spring-shrine on the eastern slope; the truthspeakers take their first oath over the water. |
| Support | **Dromcairn** | DROM-cairn | "The Ridge Cairn" — the cairn on the rise behind the landing, where the bards meet every boat and keep the island's news and its arguments. |

*Location corrected 2026-09-12: Dromcairn is the hamlet at the island's **pier on the southeast
shore**, ~0.5 km southeast of Dunadd — not "the ridge above the ford," which was a misread of the
2k map. The ford is 0.2 km from Lissban and the windmill 0.35 km east of Dunadd. Name unchanged.*

### Dál Riata features — approved 2026-09-13

**Convention: Gaelic specific + English generic.** *Owenfinn River*, *Athgorm Ford*, *Bohermara
Road*. Most are deliberately tautological — Owenfinn River is "Fair-River River" — which is exactly
how real Celtic-derived English names work (River Avon is "river river"; Bredon Hill is "hill hill
hill"). It reads authentic because it's redundant. Settlements keep the bare Gaelic; the landscape
takes the English generic, so the five settlement names stand out as the things people built.

| Headland | **Grey Head** | — | — | The NE corner, where the Sea Road turns south. |

the Benlea Mountains, past Cromcross where it turns for Carrigrua, over Athgorm Ford to Lissban.*

### The dungeon — Uaimh na Scáth

**Uaimh na Scáth** — "OO-iv na SKAW," the **Cave of Shadows**. Level **9–15**, **5-man**.

The Irish is the formal name on the map; *Cave of Shadows* is what players say. It's the one name
on Dál Riata an English speaker won't get right on sight, which is deliberate and confined to the
dungeon. *(Grammar: Irish never eclipses **s** — the pairs are b→mb, c→gc, d→nd, f→bhf, g→ng,
p→bp, t→dt — so "na dScáth" isn't valid. "na Scáth" reads as an archaic genitive plural; strict
modern Irish would be "na Scáthanna".)*

**Where:** the sea cliffs at the southern end of Camasmore Bay, below Shanderry Forest — the far
side of the island from Portcorr and the starts, so a group forms at the harbour and *crosses*
Dál Riata to reach it. The mouth is visible from the water, so players see it from the boat long
before they're 9.

**Two acts.** Fomorian raiders — sea-giants — hold the tidal caves at the front; their beached camp
outside is the level 8–10 outdoor approach, and it teaches the mechanics the dungeon tests. Behind
them, the Otherworld bleeding through: what they're squatting beside, and why they won't sleep past
the second turn. That ties the dungeon to the Sidhe across Kyleshee Strait and to the fae creature
Phase 4 puts in the northern wood.

Dál Riata now carries a complete first arc: four starts at 1–9, a raider camp at 8–10, a 5-man at
9–15, and Portcorr as the boat out.

## 8. Aethiopes — the Crusader Empire

> **Renamed from Severus, 2026-09-13 (Daniel).** The Aethiopes are the Empire's Black Africans — *Aethiopes* is the Greek and Roman name for the peoples south of Egypt. They are the Empire-born diaspora of the Crusader realm; their home kingdoms (Kush, Aksum, Mali, the Bantu) sit in the Mystic Lands' Alkebulan. ## 10. Mythic — high elves

Greek register, archaic and silvered — **approved 2026-09-12.**

**Why Greek.** The brief assigns Sidhe → Irish and Alfar → Old Norse and leaves
Mythic blank. The pattern behind those two is Daniel's 2026-07-14 direction that
each elf race pairs with its mythologically-linked human culture: Sidhe ↔ Celtic,
Alfar ↔ Germanic (*álfar* are literally the elves of Norse myth). His note
completes the set outright — "Mythic elves = high elves: start near the Hellenic
culture." Greek is the blank filled by his own rule.

The class data backs it, but narrowly. Mythic's *raw* overlap with Hellenic is
only 4 classes — tied with Sidhe and Fae, lower than Alfar (6). What matters is
*which* four. **Equal** is rollable by Hellenic, Mythic and Centaur; **Pankrator**
by Hellenic, Mythic and Gobbledrift. Among the eight Crusader human cultures only
Hellenic rolls either, and among the three elf races only Mythic does — and they
are the two overtly Greek classes in the faction (Equal = the Spartan *homoioi*,
Pankrator = pankration). Mythic's larger overlap with Alfar is generic elf
material (Dracomancer, Ranger, Truthspeaker); its small overlap with Hellenic is
the distinctive material.

**The one loose end.** The geographic half of the 2026-07-14 note did not survive
the map — Mythic Territory is the far western vastwood, about as far from Hellenic
as the continent allows. So the Mythic elves read as the older branch that
withdrew west, keeping the tongue but not the neighbourhood. That reconciliation
was invented in this session rather than directed; approved along with the names,
and free to be overwritten by better lore later.

**Settlement: Argyrion** (ar-GEER-ee-on) — "The Silver Place." The white spire
standing alone deep in the vastwood, visible from nowhere except directly below.

| Start | Name | Say it | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| All archetypes | **Anthemon** | AN-theh-mon | "The Flowering" — the tent camp on the golden hills at the wood's southern edge, where the Mythic send their young out into the light for the first time. |

  second town.
- **Slavic and Baltic** each cover all four archetypes.
- **Fae's** two markers each carry two: Healer with Frontline at Quickenwisp,
  Damage with Support at Dewglass.
- **Woodling** has no Frontline class and **Centaur** no Support class — those
  nulls are correct, not gaps.
