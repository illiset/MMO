# Dál Riata — NPC accents and cultural flavour by quarter (Daniel, 2026-09-13)

When NPCs and voices arrive, the island's four quarters each carry a British-Isles flavour:

| Quarter | Flavour | Places it covers |
|---|---|---|
| South-west | Welsh-ish | Lissban (Frontline start), Blunt Head, the coast road west of Athgorm Ford |
| South-east | British / English-ish | Dromcairn (Support start), Portcorr harbour, the Moyree Lowlands, Whitemill |
| North-east | Scottish-ish | Tobarglas (Healer start), the Bealanard Shoulder, Grey Head, Bohermara Road |
| North-west | Irish-ish | Rinnbeg (Damage start), Cuaseen, Camasmore Bay, the Fomorian coast |

Dunadd and Carrigrua sit at the centre as the mixed royal court. Place names stay Gaelic realm-wide
(canon in `names/great-north-places.md`); the quarter flavour lives in NPC voice, dialogue idiom,
personal names and small dress details, not in the map. Feeds the race bible (Celtic entry) and the
NPC lane after the MetaHuman body lane.
