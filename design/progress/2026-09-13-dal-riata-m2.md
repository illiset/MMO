# World M2 — Dál Riata: overnight build report (2026-09-13)

Built between ~03:00 and ~08:00 by the overnight session, rolling without checkpoints per
Daniel's authorisation. Level: `/Game/ThreeRealms/Zones/GN/GN_DalRiata` (new).
`Playable_FrostmarchSlice` was **not touched** — it stays the enemy lab.

**Read this first:** the island is real, walkable and named. The two things that are *not*
right are (1) the game client **GPU-crashed** on this laptop about 12 s after spawning in,
so the movement pass is bound but its behaviour is unverified, and (2) the Benlea rock

## What exists

### Phase 0 — data and terrain (DONE)
- **`data/zones/dal-riata.json`** (this repo) — the island outline as a 100-point polygon in
  world metres, the four starts, Dunadd, Carrigrua, 16 named features, four road polylines,
  the Owenfinn, the dungeon mouth and the raider camp, plus reserved **npc slots as data only**.
  Every name comes from `data/zones/great-north-names.json`; nothing was invented.
- **Scale**: the island outline was traced off `design/Mythic Earth-Great North.jpg` (8192 px)
  by colour-classifying sea vs land, taking the largest component and simplifying the boundary.
  Longest chord = **1901.5 map px**, anchored to the approved **3 km tip to tip** →
  **1 map px = 1.5777 m**. That makes the whole canvas ~12.9 km across (the earlier memory
  estimate of 14.4 km was an eyeball; this one is measured).
- **Heightmap**: `MMOKitEval/Tools/dalriata/dal_riata_2017x1261_g16.png`, 16-bit, generated by
  `gen_dal_riata.py`. Landscape is 2017 × 1261 verts, 16 × 10 components, scale (200, 200, 100)
  → **2 m per quad, 4032 m N–S × 2520 m E–W**, centred on world origin, sea level at z = 0.
  World axes: **+X = north, +Y = east**.
- **Land area 3.43 km²**; height range −60.9 m (seabed) to **+244 m** (Benlea's central summit).
- **Import verified**: 23 of 23 named key points trace to within **42 cm** of the generator's
  own value, which also proves the import orientation was right (`w02_verify_landscape.py`).

### Slope audit — roads ≤ 12° (PASS)
Measured along the graded centreline, and again on the finished surface inside the 6 m carriageway:

| road | length | max along-road grade | carriageway slope max / mean | z range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bohermara Road | 2683 m | **10.0°** | 27.1° / 6.4° | 7.7 → 94.3 m |
| Boherath Road | 534 m | **8.3°** | 10.6° / 4.8° | 42.2 → 73.8 m |
| Boherath (shore leg) | 1097 m | **7.0°** | 7.0° / 3.0° | 3.7 → 48.0 m |
| Dunadd spur | 389 m | **4.4°** | 4.7° / 1.7° | 41.1 → 51.5 m |

Verdict: **every road is ≤ 12° along its length.** The one number above 12 is Bohermara's
*cross*-slope where it is cut into Grey Head's shoulder (5.9 % of that road's carriageway
cells); it is a road cut, not a gradient the player climbs.

Island-wide: mean land slope 11.7°, 8.3 % of the land steeper than 35° (that is Benlea and the
Camasmore cliffs, by design).

### Owenfinn River and Athgorm Ford
The map does not draw a river on Dál Riata, so its course is authored from the approved names
doc: out of Shanderry Forest, south down Kingshill's western foot, across Boherath Road at
**Athgorm Ford**, then east to the sea at Portcorr. 1710 m long, 37 m of fall, water surface
forced monotonically downhill. The valley is cut **before** the roads so the road profile dips
into it, and the channel yields to the road where they cross — that is what makes the ford a
ford instead of a 5 m gorge through the carriageway. Ford water depth 1.0 m.

### Phase 1 — terrain, sea, sky, nav (DONE)
- `MI_DalRiata_Landscape`, parented to MWAM's **island** preset (not the mountain-range preset
  Frostmarch used), tuned temperate: grass everywhere, a sand band held to the shore, stones on
  slope, **snow off** (mask multiplier 0 *and* the altitude threshold pushed past the peaks).
- **Muirmore sea**: a 30 km plane at z = 0 with a dark glossy material. *No collision* — the kit
  has no swimming, and a blocking plane would break the ground probe. You can walk into the sea
  and along the seabed. Known and deliberate for v1.
- **Temperate Atlantic atmosphere** adapted from `apply_frostmarch_atmosphere.py`: warmer 6100 K
  sun, humid Mie-heavy haze, green ground albedo, maritime two-layer height fog, filmic grade.
  The sun was **re-aimed mid-build** — the first setting put it in the north-east, which threw
  the whole southern face of Benlea (the face the player sees from Dunadd) into shadow. Exactly
  the Frostmarch lake lesson. It now travels north-east, i.e. the sun sits over the Kyleshee
  Strait to the south-west.
- **Nav**: `DR_NavBounds` (a real cube brush via the toolset, 3000 × 2000 × 600 m) and
  `RecastNavMesh-Default`. **15 of 15 named points project onto the navmesh.** Path
  Lissban → Dunadd is **VALID, 506 m**. Path Rinnbeg → Dunadd came back **partial** at 1565 m —
  either recast had not finished the northern tiles when it was queried, or the Benlea range
  needs a pass. Re-check before trusting long-range mob pathing.

| the Moyree Lowlands | wheat, millet, flax | 2,159 |
| coast + cliffs | Dolomite boulders + Roman/Celtic rocks | 251 |

**The substitution, and it matters:** the Megaplant packs (English Oak, Silver Birch, Hazel,
Alder, Rowan, Hornbeam, Goat Willow, Baltic Pine, Beach Rose, Bilberry, Nettle, Wood Anemone,
Dry Reed) all landed — but they ship the finished plants as **SkeletalMeshes**, wind-animated
through their ProceduralVegetation plugin. The only StaticMeshes in them are loose branches and
leaves under `Instances/`. HISM needs a StaticMesh, so the bulk scatter uses the **Roman and
Celtic Pack's 41 static vegetation meshes** instead (Iron-Age European set — thematically right,
but lower fidelity and not Nanite). Megaplant is used for **62 hero trees** placed as
SkeletalMeshActors at the four starts, Dunadd, Carrigrua and Cromcross, where a player's camera
actually gets close. See BLOCKERS for the fix.

  **Rinnbeg** (hunters' tents on the north cape), **Tobarglas** (spring shrine, stone ring),

- **Portcorr**: a five-segment pier out over the water, crates and barrels, a house.
- **Cromcross**: the waystone at the bend.
- **Uaimh na Scáth**: the cave mouth on Camasmore Bay's southern cliffs — two big rocks framing

## BLOCKERS (ranked)

1. **The client GPU-crashes in Dál Riata on this laptop.** `LogD3D12RHI: Error: GPU crash
   detected` at frame 122, ~12 s after possession, while still compiling shaders (35 shaders,
   25 textures pending on screen), 4.56 GB of an 11.6 GB budget in use. The player *did* spawn
   correctly at Lissban with a working HUD (see the in-game capture) — the world then died under
   it. Next step: relaunch with the low profile **after** a shader pre-warm, and if it still
   dies, halve the HISM instance count and drop the 62 skeletal hero trees first (skeletal
   foliage is the most expensive thing in the level).
2. **Benlea's rock reads near-black.** The MWAM rock layer uses `TEX_MWAM_Rock_col`; pushing
   `MW_RockColorCorrection` from 1.0 → 1.55 → 3.4 barely moved it, so it is probably not the
   colour correction but the layer's blend or the preset's rock roughness. Next step: open the
   island preset in the material editor and look at what feeds Rock, or swap the rock texture
   for one of the vault's Quixel rock surfaces.
3. **Camera zoom clamp — the blocker is broken open, the edit is not made.** This has been
   "GUI-locked, impossible from script" since 2026-07-06. It is not any more: the official MCP
   toolset `editor_toolset.toolsets.blueprint.BlueprintTools` has `read_graph_dsl` /
   `write_graph_dsl`, and reading
   `/Game/MMOKit/MMO_Logic/Blueprints/Camera/BP_WowStyleCameraComponent.BP_WowStyleCameraComponent:EventGraph`
   returns the whole graph as an S-expression. **The literals are right there:**

   ```
   (event EventZoomInOut (X)
     (Variables|Zoom|SetZoomAlpha …)
     (Variables|Zoom|SetZoomFrom (Class|SpringArmComponent|GetTargetArmLength …))
     (Variables|Zoom|SetZoomTo (Math|Float|Clamp(Float)
        (+ (* X 100.000000) (Variables|Zoom|GetZoomTo)) 100.000000 1200.000000)))
   ```

   So today's values are **step 100, min 100, max 1200**, and BeginPlay already sets the default
   arm length to **900** (Daniel's hand edit, confirmed in the graph). The change Daniel asked
   for is exactly three numbers: `* X 100` → `* X 120`, `100.000000` → `250.000000`,
   `1200.000000` → `1600.000000`.

   **I did not make it.** `write_graph_dsl` replaces the *whole* graph, and the two reads of the
   same logic (EventGraph vs the generated ubergraph) already disagree on argument order in the
   `Math|Interpolation|Ease` node — so the round-trip is not provably lossless, and this is the
   camera Blueprint. Rewriting it unattended, minutes before Daniel woke, was not a risk worth
   taking for three numbers. The asset IS tracked in git, so the write is revertible: say the
   word and it is a five-minute job with a walk-test check after it.
4. **Rinnbeg → Dunadd pathfinds only partially.** Re-run `w08_nav.py` on a fully built navmesh
   before blaming the terrain.
5. **Megaplant is skeletal-only.** Either run the editor's "Convert to Static Mesh" over the
   ~50 species variants (then re-run `gen_scatter.py` with the Megaplant paths restored — the
   species table is still in git history), or adopt their ProceduralVegetation plugin.

Two engine facts worth keeping: UE 5.8 Python has **no `Actor.add_component_by_class`** — the
`SubobjectDataSubsystem` (`k2_gather_subobject_data_for_instance` → `add_new_subobject`) is the
route that makes a real, saved HISM on a placed actor. And
`NavigationSystemV1.project_point_to_navigation` returns a bare `Vector` in 5.8, not the
`(bool, Vector)` tuple the 5.7 scripts unpacked.
