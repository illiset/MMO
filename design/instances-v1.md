# Instances v1 — terms and level bands (Daniel, 2026-09-14)

## Terms (decided 2026-09-14)
- **Dungeon** = the grind instance (DAoC "Catacombs" type): population-instanced, open 24/7, no run limits, camps held
  by groups, respawns that can wipe you, great XP, decent loot.
- **Lair** = the loot 5-man (WoW-style set piece): cleared in legs, bosses, a final boss that counts the run, 5 runs
  per day per character (counted on the final boss kill; wipes do not burn a run; capped players may enter with friends
  but get no boss loot/XP), a per-hour instance-creation cap as plumbing only. "5-man loot is achievable but not
  guaranteed in a day."
- **Raid** = 50+ only, weekly lockouts. Added later.

Bands overlap by a level at each seam on purpose: a group on the top level of one band can still run it, or step up.
Every band should exist as at least one Dungeon and one Lair somewhere in each realm eventually; the first zone (Dál
Riata) carries band 1 as a Lair (Uaimh na Scáth), a Dungeon still to be sited and named by Daniel, and a second 5-man
he will establish. The Early Access cap is 30 (bands 1–5 reachable); destination cap 50.

## Raids
50+ only. Weekly lockouts. Not before the level-50 destination cap is live.
