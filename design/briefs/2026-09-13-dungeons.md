# Brief: dungeon design v1 — two dungeon types (for an Opus design session)

**Owner:** Daniel. **Session model:** Opus. **Writes to:** this design repo only
(`design/dungeons-v1.md`). Engine work follows later in the dungeon lane.

## Daniel's direction (2026-09-13, verbatim in substance)
Dungeon design is more than a map; it is gameplay and leveling. Mythic Earth will have BOTH:

1. **Grind dungeons — DAoC "Catacombs" type.** Instanced by population: several groups share
   an instance, and when it gets too packed a new instance opens for the next groups. A party
   finds a spot, holds it, keeps it cleared and grinds from it. Far more dangerous — respawns can
   wipe you. Decent loot, great XP, if you don't die.
2. **Set-piece dungeons — WoW-style 5-mans.** Clear to a point; it stays clear unless you take
   forever. Beat the boss of that leg, clear to the next boss, possibly multiple paths; a final
   boss; earlier decisions can decide whether an extra/secret boss exists after the final one.
   Limited runs per day, or a lockout, WoW-Classic style (daily/weekly).

## What already constrains this
- `design/progression-v1.md` §6: Uaimh na Scáth (Dál Riata, 5-player, level 9–15) sits inside the
  free trial at its 9–10 band and must be clearable by a level 9–10 group; its 9–15 band is a
  content band, not a difficulty ramp. That makes it the set-piece type with legs by level.
- `design/names/great-north-places.md` §1: two acts inside the cave (Fomorian tidal caves in
  front, the Otherworld behind) — a natural place for a branching path and a secret boss.
- Roadmap Phase 3: the first dungeon is the thesis; Phase 5 wants a second dungeon.
- Death: XP debt, no de-level (progression-v1 §4) — grind-dungeon danger costs time, never progress.
- Pillars: PvE-first, no microtransactions; the two dungeon types must each have a reason to exist
  (set-piece = bosses, story, gear; grind = XP, farming, holding a camp as a group skill).

## Questions to settle (decisions, not options)
1. Which type is Uaimh na Scáth, and does its Otherworld wing become the island's grind space?
2. Set-piece rules: leg structure by level band, respawn timer for "cleared stays cleared", how
   choices gate the secret boss, and the daily/weekly limit (proposal to test: no daily cap on
   entry, a per-hour instance cap like Classic's 5/hour, and boss loot/XP diminishing after N
   clears per day so farmers move to the grind dungeon).
3. Grind rules: instance population cap and when a new instance opens, camp mechanics (respawn
   waves, patrols, "pulling" as the skill), XP and loot rates relative to open-world and 5-mans.
4. Group size assumptions for each (5 for set-piece; grind dungeons may host several groups).
5. Where the first grind dungeon lives (second zone? under Benlea? the Otherworld wing?).
6. What the engine needs: an instance manager that spawns a private server per group (set-piece)
   and a population-based instance spawner (grind); lockout/limit storage on the character.

## Daniel's lean on limits (2026-09-13, treat as the starting decision)
- **Grind dungeons: open 24/7, no limits.** Their loot is weaker than the boss 5-mans, so there is
  nothing to gate; population instancing handles crowding.
- **Boss 5-mans: 5 runs per day** so a player has a real shot at the drops they want.
- Claude's refinements to test: count a run when the final boss dies (wipes don't burn a run); a
  capped player may still enter with friends but gets no boss loot/XP; keep a separate technical
  cap on instance creation per hour (Classic's 5/hour) as anti-farm/anti-load, not as design;
  weekly lockouts are for raids only; tune boss drop rates against the 5/day budget as one dial.
  (History check: Classic 5-mans were actually unlimited apart from the 5-instances-per-hour cap;
  daily lockouts arrived with TBC heroics — 5/day sits comfortably between the two.)

- **Agreed by Daniel 2026-09-13:** runs count on the final boss kill; capped players may still enter with
  friends (no boss loot/XP); weekly lockouts = raids only; the daily cap and drop rates are one dial with the
  principle **"5-man loot is achievable but not guaranteed in a day."** The per-hour instance-creation cap is
  plumbing (anti reset-farming, server protection), invisible to normal play.
