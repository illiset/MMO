Order below is the priority order, not a single night's scope.
1. **Terrain integrity**: revert the road/river trench cuts everywhere except the forest hollow-way; roads benched as
   shelves (≤ 2.5 m cut/fill, wide blended shoulders, ≤ 12° along, level across); ridge-crest rounding; the pockmarked
   lowland smoothed; coast re-cut (cliffs only at headlands + Benlea's sea faces, shingle/boulder/strand/machair elsewhere,
   every line noise-broken); the carrigrua↔dromcairn sightline regression fixed and the full audit re-run; a natural wooded hill raised NE of Lissban so Kingshill's crown and the castle are invisible from inside the village (item 38), the Boherath bending round its foot.
2. **Polygons**: Nanite landscape ON; the 2 m/quad regeneration started in the background (or on the PC); LOD
   distribution raised in the play profile (done); prove with Daniel's two red-line poses.
3. **Forest (standing order)**: open woodland the norm (150–250/ha), dense pockets rare and deliberate, 20–30 glades /
   points of interest with their own dressing (grind camps, ruin, pond, waystone, bandit camp, the halfway rest on the
   forest road with a table and merchants); canopy 4x higher (branches ≥ 15–20 m, ceiling 25–40 m), giants 1-in-5 at 2x
   trunk, no clones (variants × scale × tint), bare trunks (height mask), understory + shrubs everywhere, snags/logs;
   edge = 100–150 m scatter then done, duff derived from real canopy, colour transitions 50–150 m; Norway Spruce if Daniel
   adds it.
4. **Ground life**: dense meadow cover in every few metres (tufts, wildflowers, nettle, bilberry, heather, gorse, rocks)
   in drifts, thicker along fences/hedges/forest edge; the road surface de-tiled (second texture, ruts, edge litter).
5. **Water**: lake clipped to its basin contour (no ellipse) with reeds/shingle; swim volumes over lake/sea/deep river +
   underwater post-process + two-sided surface + lake bed; rivers as splines with flowing water, meanders, dressed banks,
   the waterfall with pool + mist; sea Atlantic grey-green with shore foam; trees cleared from channels.
6. **Lissban to spec**: one level pad for the whole enclosure, grass with dirt paths E–W and to every door; EAST gate with
   a baffle screen + WEST gate, both F-operated with auto-close; hut collision from the real walls and real doors (2 m
   doors), wooden floors, the buried hut re-seated, trough dug up, pen closed except its gap; fields all dirt to the
   fence with a hard edge, crops clipped to the fence polygon, farm tools; the standing stones deleted; villagers in
   place; capture set + walk.
7. **Creatures**: mobs must deal damage (timer-based melee on the server), wolf HP +40% (done in data) then balance to
   "auto-attack alone loses to a yellow"; animal locomotion via the pack's ABPs / stride-matched rates with blends
   (no twig legs, no headbanging) — judged on a capture sequence; turn rates; habitats re-checked after terrain changes.
8. **HUD**: con ring = flat yellow with a translucent yellow disc, colour by level difference (grey/green/blue/yellow/
   orange/red/purple), seated on the ground; XP bar built from design/reference/ui/experience-bar.png; mouse sensitivity
   (X/Y, invert) in the ESC settings; the kit's hover decal restyled or off.
9. **Weather/fog**: overcast baseline (~70%), volumetric fog pooling in valleys, thicker north, clear pockets on some
   coasts, no white-out; judged against the PNW photos.
10. **Audits + report**: HISM/foliage-instance grounding audit island-wide; overlap audit; sightline audit; captures from
    the standard poses + Daniel's red-line poses; design/progress/2026-09-15-night4.md; both repos pushed; memory.
