## 3. TERRAIN
19. Revert the road/river TRENCHES everywhere except the forest hollow-way (Daniel likes that one); roads elsewhere sit on the
    ground: ≤ 2.5 m cut/fill, shoulders ≥ 25 m, side slopes ≤ 25°, no rectangular footprints; where more cut is needed the road
    moves, not the hill.
20. Mountain roads as a BENCH: 3.5–4 m level shelf across the slope, uphill cut face ≤ 2.5 m, downhill boulder kerb, ≤ 12° along
    grade; keep the rock-face look.
21. POLYGON SILHOUETTES (critical): Nanite landscape ON; regenerate at 2 m/quad (background/PC); ridge-crest rounding over 8–12 m;
    LOD distribution raised (done in the play profile); prove on Daniel's two red-line poses.
22. Pockmarked lowland smoothed (erosion pits removed, big rolls kept; bare dirt only on real slopes).
23. Coast: cliffs ONLY at the headlands (Blunt Head, Grey Head) and Benlea's sea faces; elsewhere shingle, boulder shore, rock
    platforms, pocket strands, dune/machair grass, grassy banks; every line (shoreline, terrace edge, cliff tops) noise-broken at
    50/200/800 m; the terrace blended 60–120 m into the slope.
24. Terrain that FRAMES (Durotar rule): ridges, tree walls and hedgerow lines enclose areas; the open plain goes away; Lissban
    itself stays flat.
25. Ground-colour transitions 50–150 m everywhere (duff→grass, grass→heath/rock at Benlea's foot); no contrast lines.
26. Benlea summit: bare rock + scree + boulder fields above 420 m, heath band below, scree aprons under cliffs; mountains must
    differ from each other (reference screenshots).
27. Sightline regressions: carrigrua↔dromcairn (−0.4 m) fixed; audit extended with landform checks; re-run after every terrain
    change.

## 4. VEGETATION AND GROUND LIFE
29. Forest standing order: OPEN woodland is the norm (150–250 stems/ha), DENSE pockets rare and deliberate; 20–30 glades /
    points of interest with their own dressing (grind camps, a ruin, a pond, the Cromcross waystone glade, bandit camps, the
    forest-road halfway rest with table/benches/fire/cart/merchants/signpost); game trails between them.
30. Canopy 4x higher: lowest branches ≥ 15–20 m, ceiling 25–40 m, thicker branches/needles; bare trunks below (height mask);
    giants 1-in-5 at 2x trunk; NO CLONES (3–4 variants × scale 0.8–1.3 × per-instance tint); Norway Spruce when Daniel adds it.
31. Forest edge: scatter 100–150 m past the core then end within ~50 m; duff/moss derived from the ACTUAL canopy, feathered;
    no straight edges anywhere (fields excepted).
32. Shrubs everywhere: bilberry, nettle, anemone, hazel/rowan/birch saplings, gorse, heather; snags and fallen logs.
33. Ground life to the reference bar: dense varied grass cover every few metres in drifts (tufts, wildflowers, clover, dry
    stalks), thicker along fences/hedges/forest edge; rocks and boulders of many sizes with lichen/moss.
34. Road surfaces de-tiled: second dirt/gravel texture, wheel ruts and puddles, leaf litter and stones at the edges, grass
    creeping in.
35. Rest stops every ~1.5–2 km on long roads (shrine, well, herder's hut, waystone).
36. HISM/foliage-instance grounding audit island-wide (the floating Dromcairn boulder); overlap audit after every pass.
